Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

Last Updated: October 9, 2026 • Version 0.1.0

Swapwear ("we", "our", "us") operates the Swapwear mobile application - a pre-loved fashion marketplace to buy, sell and swap clothing. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account & Profile Information (you provide)

  • Name, email address, phone number, bio, address / township
  • Profile photo, product listing photos and videos
  • Seller verification: NRC front / back images, approval status
  • OAuth data if you sign in with Google: Google ID, display name, email, profile image

B. Transaction & Marketplace Activity

  • Listings, orders, order status, payment method, reward points, sell count, trust level, role (buyer/seller)
  • Messages, reviews, reports, recent searches, language and theme preferences
  • Two-factor authentication data if enabled

C. Device & Usage Data (automatic)

  • Firebase FCM push token, notification interaction, crash / diagnostic logs, app performance
  • IP address, device model, OS version, app version, network state, locale (EN / Myanmar)
  • Cached images and local preferences stored on-device via GetStorage
Sensitive ID Notice: We only request NRC images to verify sellers, prevent fraud and build trust. NRC images are never shown publicly and are only visible to authorized reviewers. You may request deletion after verification - see Section 9.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage accounts, authenticate via Firebase Auth
  • List, search, order, deliver and support second-hand transactions
  • Verify seller identity (NRC review), calculate trust level / rewards, prevent fraud
  • Send transactional and promotional push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging + flutter_local_notifications
  • Remember theme, language, recent searches locally for better UX
  • Improve stability, security, analytics and customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Sharing of Information

  • With other users: Only your public profile (name, photo, bio, listings, trust level, sell count) and listing details. Your phone, email, address and NRC are never public - shared with counterparty only after order confirmation for delivery.
  • Service providers: Hosting, Firebase, notification delivery, strictly to operate the app.
  • Legal: If required by Myanmar law, court order, or to prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Business transfer: If merged/acquired, users will be notified.

6. Local Storage & Cookies

Swapwear is a mobile app and does not use web cookies. We store auth token, user profile, app settings, recent searches on-device using GetStorage. Clearing app data / uninstalling removes local data. Cached product images may remain until cache cleared.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data retained while account is active + up to 2 years after for fraud, tax, dispute purposes.
  • NRC images retained until verification + dispute period, then anonymized or deleted on request if law permits.
  • Order logs retained up to 5 years where required for accounting.
  • Push tokens deleted on logout / uninstall.

8. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS, Firebase secure auth, access controls and limited NRC access. No system is 100% secure - please use strong passwords, enable 2FA, and do not share OTPs. Report abuse to support below.

9. Your Rights - Access, Correction, Deletion

  • View/edit profile in-app: Profile > Edit Profile, Settings > Language / Theme
  • Request export, correction, or deletion of account + NRC images via email below
  • Withdraw consent for marketing notifications anytime in system settings
  • We respond within 30 days. Deletion removes listings, profile and verification images from active systems; order records required by law may be anonymized.

To delete account: In-app if available, or email support@swapwear.com with subject "Delete My Account" from your registered email/phone.

10. Children's Privacy

Swapwear is not for children under 13 (or under 16 without parental consent). We do not knowingly collect child data. Parent/guardian discovering such data should contact us for deletion.

11. International Transfers

Data may be processed on Firebase/Google servers outside Myanmar. By using Swapwear you consent to such transfer with appropriate safeguards.
