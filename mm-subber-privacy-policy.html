Privacy Policy

Welcome to MM Subber (Myanmar Subtitle Editor). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you process using our mobile application.

1

Overview & Data Ownership

MM Subber is a local-first mobile subtitle editing, translation, and management tool designed for Myanmar translators and creators.

We believe your content belongs to you. The application operates primarily offline on your device, processing subtitle files (`.srt`, `.vtt`), media previews, and dictionary entries locally.

🔒 Key Takeaway: We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell your personal information or the contents of your subtitle files on any centralized servers.

2

Information Handled by the App

Here is a detailed breakdown of how data is handled within MM Subber:

  • Local Subtitle & Media Files: When you open or edit a subtitle file (`.srt`, `.vtt`) or load a video for sync preview, the app processes these files entirely on your local device storage.
  • App Preferences & Settings: Your customized preferences (such as app theme, font size, Burmese typography settings, and recent file locations) are saved locally on your device using encrypted/local storage key-value pairs.
  • Translation Requests: When you manually use the online translation feature (English to Myanmar), text snippets of selected subtitle lines are sent via secure HTTPS to third-party translation API services. These text snippets contain no personal identifiers.
  • Offline Dictionary: The built-in English to Myanmar dictionary uses a bundled local database on your device and requires no internet connection.
3

Device Permissions Required

To provide full subtitle editing functionality, MM Subber requests the following device permissions:

  • Storage / Media Files Access: Required to allow you to browse, import, edit, sync, and export subtitle files and media previews stored on your device.
  • Internet Access: Required only for optional online subtitle lookup and fetching translation query results when you choose to use the online translation tools.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

4

Third-Party Services

MM Subber may interface with third-party web services strictly to fulfill user-requested features:

  • Translation APIs: Processing text translation queries (English ↔ Myanmar). These requests carry no user account details or personal metadata.
  • Online Subtitle Providers: Fetching publicly available open subtitle files when explicitly searched by the user in the Subtitle Explorer section.

We do not use third-party tracking scripts, ad networks, or user behavioral profiling analytics.

5

Data Security & Children's Privacy

Security: Because your data and files remain locally on your mobile device, the security of your files relies on your device's built-in security controls.

Children's Privacy: MM Subber does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. The app is a professional utility tool for translators and content creators of all ages.

6

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
